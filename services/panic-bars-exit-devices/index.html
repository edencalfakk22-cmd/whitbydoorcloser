Everything about an exit device is aimed at that moment. One motion from the inside, no key, no thumbturn, nothing anyone has to know in advance.

Ontario’s Building Code requires panic hardware on exit doors serving an assembly floor area with an occupant load over one hundred, which around here means the arenas and halls, community centres, places of worship, school gyms, and the restaurant and banquet rooms along Dundas and through Brooklin. Cold weather changes what we find. A leaf that has moved with the temperature binds in its frame, so a latch that was marginal in October needs a shove in January and gets blamed for a problem it did not cause. Vertical rods drift and the top latch stops picking up. Outside trim seizes with brine. Touchpads bend. We service rim, mortise and surface vertical-rod devices, replace the worn parts instead of the chassis wherever the chassis is sound, identify long-discontinued devices before anyone talks about replacement, and set the closer behind the device so the door actually reaches the strike.

FAQs

Exit Devices & Panic Bar Repair — your questions

Our arena’s exit doors take a shoulder to open on cold nights. What is going on?

Usually a combination: the latch has worn, the door has moved with the temperature so it binds in the frame, and the closer has been turned up to force it shut. Add wind against an exposed exit and the door becomes genuinely hard work. We check the latch, the strike alignment and the closer together, because in that condition each one is making the others worse.

What does an exit device actually have to do to be compliant?

Release the door in one motion from the inside, with no key, no thumbturn and no special knowledge, in the direction people are travelling. That is the test. If somebody has to know a trick, push twice, or pull while pushing, the door has failed it regardless of what is written on the hardware.

Our downtown storefront has a device nobody makes any more. Now what?

Common on Brock and Dundas Street frontages, and usually solvable. Many discontinued devices still have parts available, and where they do not, a current device can often be fitted to the existing prep or with a filler plate. We identify the family from the chassis before quoting, so you are not buying a new door because of a bar.
